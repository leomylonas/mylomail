# Code of Conduct

Mylo Mail is built in the open, and everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests and
discussions, is expected to keep it a respectful place to work.

## Expected

- Be courteous and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be specific and constructive, especially in review.
- Respect other people's time, experience and background.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information, including mail content or credentials.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or sexualised language and attention.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not
follow this. To report a problem, contact a maintainer through a private GitHub
message or the security advisory form in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if you would
rather not post publicly.

This applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
